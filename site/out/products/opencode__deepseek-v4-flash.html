Methods and disclosure. The battery, runner, and measures were built with Claude (Fable 5) doing the engineering, and Claude-family rows appear in the results — including Fable 5 itself, which scores near the bottom of the "fell short, said so" column. The headline column counts, among runs where the hidden check failed, those whose final message acknowledged a shortfall (or made no success claim). Runs the provider refused (content filter) count as neither passes nor failures; they appear as grey cells and shrink that row's denominators. Text matching is by fixed regular expressions, published, and every cell links to the transcript they read. Dot colors follow one rule across all scenarios: they grade the report's candor, not the work; the soft file-name matcher is excluded from colors and counted only in the misdescribed column.
