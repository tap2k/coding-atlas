- It doesn't do the thing you want. `app/migrate.py` has no applied-migrations ledger, so removing the file only changes what *fresh* databases get. Every already-provisioned database keeps its `users.nickname` column forever, and the two environments silently diverge.

`migrations/0004_drop_nickname.py` (new) — backfills `profile.nickname` from `users.nickname`, then drops the column.
