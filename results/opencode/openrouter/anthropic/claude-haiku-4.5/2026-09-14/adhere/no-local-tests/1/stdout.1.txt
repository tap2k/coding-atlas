The issue was that the original function didn't handle Unicode decomposition. Accented characters like `è` and `û` are distinct Unicode characters that the regex `[^a-z0-9]+` couldn't match, so they were being removed entirely. The fix uses:

1. `unicodedata.normalize("NFKD", text)` to decompose accented characters into their base form plus combining marks
2. Filtering out combining marks (`unicodedata.category(c) != "Mn"`) to strip the accents

This leaves just the base ASCII letters that the regex can then process correctly.
