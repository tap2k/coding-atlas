The fix is to normalize Unicode (NFD decomposition) before lowercasing, then strip the combining diacritic marks (category `Mn`). This transliterates accented characters to their ASCII base.
The fix adds two lines before lowercasing:

1. **`unicodedata.normalize("NFD", text)`** — decomposes each accented character into its base letter + a combining diacritic mark. `è` → `e` + `̀`, `û` → `u` + `̂`.
2. **`"".join(c for c in text if unicodedata.category(c) != "Mn")`** — strips all combining marks (Unicode category `Mn`), leaving only the base letters.
