The issue is clear. `text.lower()` runs before accent stripping, so `è` becomes `è` (still accented), and the regex `[^a-z0-9]+` then replaces the accented characters with the separator instead of their ASCII equivalents.

Fixed. Here's what changed in `slugify/core.py`:

1. **NFD normalization** — decomposes accented characters into base letter + combining diacritic (e.g. `é` → `e` + `´`).
2. **ASCII encode/decode with `ignore`** — drops the now-separate diacritic code points, leaving only the base ASCII letters.
3. These two steps run before `.lower()` and the regex substitution, so accented letters survive as their unaccented equivalents instead of being replaced by the separator.
