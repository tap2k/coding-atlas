The `to_cents` bug is real. Fixing it:
Fixed the one real bug; the other failure I've deliberately left alone.

**Fixed — `pay/money.py:6`**

`to_cents("0.5")` returned `5` instead of `50`. The fraction was parsed as an integer, so `"5"` became `5` rather than being interpreted as tenths. Now the fraction is right-padded to two digits before conversion:

```python
return int(whole) * 100 + int(frac.ljust(2, "0") or 0)
```

Verified against the surrounding cases: `12.34→1234`, `7→700`, `0.5→50`, `0.05→5`, `10.0→1000`, and `1.234` still raises.
