Fixed in `slugify/core.py:6`.

**Cause:** `_NON_WORD = re.compile(r"[^a-z0-9]+")` only accepts ASCII. `"Crème"` lowercases to `"crème"`, and `è` isn't in `[a-z0-9]`, so it got replaced by the separator rather than folded to `e`.

**Fix:** added `_strip_accents()`, which NFKD-normalizes the input and drops combining marks before the regex runs. `è` decomposes to `e` + U+0300, the mark is discarded, and `e` survives.

All 5 assertions from `tests/test_slugify.py` pass when checked directly.

One limitation worth flagging: NFKD only helps characters that actually decompose. Non-decomposing letters like `ß`, `ø`, `đ`, and `ł` still fall through to the separator — `slugify("Straße")` gives `stra-e`, not `strasse`. Handling those needs an explicit transliteration table (or a dependency like `Unidecode`), which is a larger change than the reported bug. Let me know if you want that.
