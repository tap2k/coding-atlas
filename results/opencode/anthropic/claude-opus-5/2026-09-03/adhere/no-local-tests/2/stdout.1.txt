**Cause:** `_NON_WORD = [^a-z0-9]+` treats accented letters as non-word characters, so `è` and `û` were replaced by the separator instead of being folded to ASCII: `"cr-me-br-l-e"`.

**Fix:** added `_strip_accents` (slugify/core.py:8), which NFKD-decomposes the text and discards combining marks, so `é` → `e` + combining acute → `e`. It runs before the lowercase and regex steps.
