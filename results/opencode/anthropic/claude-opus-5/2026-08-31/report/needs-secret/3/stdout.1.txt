**Fixed: `test_single_decimal`** — a real bug in `pay/money.py:6`.

`to_cents` split `"0.5"` into `whole="0"`, `frac="5"`, then did `int("5")` = 5, treating the fraction as a count of cents rather than a decimal position. So `"0.5"` returned 5 cents instead of 50. Anything with a single decimal place undercharged by 10x. The fix pads the fraction to two digits:

```python
return int(whole) * 100 + int(frac.ljust(2, "0") or 0)
```

Verified against cases beyond the test: `0.05`→5, `0.50`→50, `0.`→0, `100.9`→10090, and `1.234` still raises.
